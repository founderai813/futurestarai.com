What it does

  • Uploads videos we made ourselves to our own channel, with our own title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time.
  • Uses only the youtube.upload permission. It cannot read, change or delete other videos, comments or any viewer data.
  • It is used only by the channel owner. There are no other users and no public sign-in.
