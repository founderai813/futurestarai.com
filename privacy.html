Privacy Policy

FutureStar AI · Last updated: November 22, 2026 · FutureStar AI Uploader · Terms of Service · Home

This policy explains how FutureStar AI ("we") handles information in its private YouTube uploading tool (the "Uploader") and on futurestarai.com.

1. What the Uploader does

The Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of FutureStar AI. It uploads videos that we produced ourselves to our own YouTube channels, together with the title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time we wrote. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. YouTube API Services

The Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, we agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What information we access

  • The Uploader requests only the youtube.upload permission. It can upload videos and thumbnails to our own channels.
  • It cannot read, change or delete existing videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or any other YouTube data.
  • It does not access any viewer's or any other person's Google or YouTube account or data.

4. How we use and store information

  • The only data sent to YouTube is the video files and text we created.
  • The access credentials for our own channel are stored encrypted (GitHub Actions secrets) and are used only to upload our videos.
  • The Uploader stores a record of the video ID and upload time of our own uploads so the same video is not uploaded twice. No personal data of other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking for the Uploader.

5. Website visitors

futurestarai.com is a static website. It does not require login and does not ask visitors for personal information. Free tools on the site run in your browser and do not send what you type to us unless a tool clearly says so.

6. Revoking access

Access granted to the Uploader can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

7. Data deletion

  • When access is revoked, the Uploader can no longer connect to YouTube. We then delete the stored access credentials within 7 days.
  • The upload records (video ID and upload time) are kept only as long as needed to avoid duplicate uploads, and are deleted within 30 days after we stop using the Uploader.
  • To ask us to delete any information, email the contact address below. We will reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Videos on YouTube can be deleted at any time in YouTube Studio: https://studio.youtube.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
